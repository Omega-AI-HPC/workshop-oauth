Workshop is an AI assistant with a visual canvas. Connect Slack and ask what your team decided, what changed this week or what is still open. Workshop reads what you can already see and answers on the canvas.

What you can do with Slack

Summarize

"Summarize the last 20 messages in #release." Workshop reads the channel and writes a short summary.

Search and chart

"Find messages about the launch this week and show them in a table." Results land on your canvas.

Draft, then confirm

Workshop drafts a message for you. Nothing is posted until you confirm it with a button, a typed "send" or your voice.

Your data stays with you

Workshop runs on your computer. Your Slack token and messages stay on your machine and are never used to train models.

Install

  1. Download the latest Workshop release and start it on your computer (instructions are included in the download).
  2. Open Workshop, go to Settings > Slack and click Connect with Slack.
  3. Choose your workspace on Slack's page and click Allow. You are done: ask Workshop a question about your Slack.

Sending messages is off by default. Turn it on in Settings > Slack if you want Workshop to post drafts you confirm. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, which revokes Workshop's access.

AI disclaimer

Workshop uses a large language model (OpenAI) to understand requests and write answers. AI output can be inaccurate, so check important results. Workshop never posts to Slack without your explicit confirmation.
