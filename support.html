Common questions

Connect with Slack shows an error

Click Connect with Slack again from Workshop Settings. If your workspace requires admin approval for new apps, ask a Slack admin to approve Workshop.

Workshop says it cannot read a channel

Workshop can only read channels you can see yourself. For private channels, you must be a member.

How do I send messages?

Turn on Allow sending Slack messages in Settings > Slack, then click Reconnect once. Workshop drafts a message and posts it only after you confirm it.

How do I remove Workshop?

Click Disconnect in Settings > Slack. This revokes Workshop's access at Slack and deletes the token from your computer. You can also remove the app in your Slack workspace settings.

What happens to my data?

Your Slack token and data stay on your computer. See the privacy policy.
